Sentinel table — 10 design directions

Same findings, same dark Plasma look. The shared premise: drop the per-column filter row and the severity chips (they duplicate the cards) and keep the three answers visible — how bad (score + band colour), what is vulnerable (package + version), and what introduced it (origin). Detector, confidence, tracker and fix detail move to hover or expand.

Recommendation: implement 02 (two-line rows) as the default, offer 04 (group by origin) as an optional switch, and use 10's expansion for the detail. Replace the header/filter row with one search field plus a single “Filters” popover. The score + colour is enough for “how bad” once the chips are gone.
