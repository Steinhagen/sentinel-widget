Reading the variants: V1 and V5 keep the row exactly as it is today and only add a cue, so the vendor → product lives in the tooltip — fine for spotting, weak for clearing a cluster. V2 keeps a uniform height but the amber suffix competes with the origin for the same line and ellipsizes first. V3 pays 14 px on review rows only and is the only one that shows both the origin and the CVE identity at once. V4 makes “review” loud but hides the thing that makes it actionable.
