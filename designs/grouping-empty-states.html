Sentinel — what should the grouping control say when nothing is grouped?

Five directions for the flat-list state. The toolbar, cards and table stay unchanged; only the grouping control and its active-mode treatment differ.

Recommendation: 01 — current-state split button. It always names the state (“Flat” / “Package”), keeps one compact control, and preserves the existing menu. 03 is the cleanest fallback if you prefer a single menu button over a split control.
