Progress indicators — 5 directions

Same header, same dark Plasma context, five different ways to show post-scan/tracker progress. The current circular spinner is replaced in each mockup by the indicator highlighted on the right.

Recommendation: use 01 (slim inline bar + count) for the tracker, where the total is known, and fall back to its indeterminate shimmer while the scan itself runs. If you want the most minimal look, 05 (top-edge line) is the least intrusive. 04 saves the most space but makes the action button do double duty.
